Smart Surplus Food Redistribution

Rescue Food. Reduce Waste. Feed Communities.

RescueMeal connects people and organizations with surplus food to NGOs that can collect and redistribute it to communities in need.

Donate Food Find Food

Why RescueMeal?

Turning surplus food into a useful community resource through a simple digital platform.

⚠️

The Problem

Usable food can be wasted because donors and organizations that need food are not always connected in time.

  • Surplus food may be discarded.
  • Manual coordination can be slow.
  • Food may expire before collection.
💡

Our Solution

RescueMeal provides a centralized platform for managing the complete surplus food redistribution process.

  • Donors can list available food.
  • NGOs can discover and claim food.
  • Donation status can be tracked.

How RescueMeal Works

A simple three-step process from surplus food to collection.

STEP 01
🍲

Donate Food

Donors add details such as food name, quantity, type, expiry time and pickup address.

STEP 02
🤝

NGO Claims

NGOs can view available donations and claim food suitable for their needs.

STEP 03
✅

Food Collected

After pickup, the donation is marked as collected and the system updates its status.

Who Can Use RescueMeal?

The platform provides separate functions for each type of user.

👤

Donors

Individuals, restaurants and organizations can share their surplus food.

🏢

NGOs

NGOs can find suitable food donations, claim them and manage collection.

🛡️

Administrators

Administrators can monitor users, NGOs, donations and system statistics.

Key Features

Core functions included in the RescueMeal platform.

👤

Donor Dashboard

Add food donations and view the history and current status of submitted food.

🏢

NGO Dashboard

Browse available donations and manage claimed and collected food.

📦

Donation Management

Store food details including quantity, type, expiry time and pickup address.

🔄

Status Tracking

Track donations from Available to Claimed and finally Collected.

⏱️

Expiry Awareness

Preparation and expiry information helps support timely food collection.
