About RescueMeal

A smart platform designed to connect surplus food with organizations that can redistribute it to communities in need.

What is RescueMeal?

RescueMeal is a web-based surplus food redistribution platform developed to reduce food wastage and make better use of excess food.

The system connects food donors such as households, restaurants and organizations with registered NGOs that can collect and distribute available food.

By bringing donors and NGOs together on one platform, RescueMeal makes the process of donating, claiming and collecting food more organized.

How RescueMeal Works

A simple process for turning surplus food into meaningful community support.

STEP 01
🍲

Donate

Donors add available surplus food, quantity, type, expiry information and pickup details.

STEP 02
🤝

Connect

NGOs can view available donations and claim food that is suitable for their needs.

STEP 03
✅

Collect

After pickup, the NGO marks the donation as collected and the system updates the status.

Key Features

Core features implemented in the RescueMeal platform.

👤

Donor Management

Donors can register, add surplus food and track their submitted donations.

🏢

NGO Management

NGOs can view available food, claim donations and manage collections.

📦

Donation Tracking

Donations move through statuses such as Available, Claimed, Collected and Expired.

🛡️

Admin Monitoring

Administrators can monitor users, NGOs, donations and system statistics.

🤖

AI Demand Prediction

Machine-learning capabilities can support prediction of future food demand.

⏱️

Expiry Awareness

Food preparation and expiry information helps support timely collection.
